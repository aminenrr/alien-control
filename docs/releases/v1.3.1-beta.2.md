**Alien Controls 1.3.1-beta.2**: a redesigned Lights menu, rounder Classic notifications and a shorter Pill. Untested pre-release.

### Changes
- **Lights menu**: a laptop button replaces Off. Its tiles show what's **on**: tap Keyboard, Rear ring or Alienhead to switch it. The fourth tile is **All off** while anything is on, **All on** once everything is off. The menu opens above the button; Esc, a click outside or the button closes it. The power button light is never touched.
- Fix: one click could switch a light on and straight back off.
- Fix: after turning the keyboard off and on, the white status keys (F1, F12, mute, mic, right Win) stayed dark until you clicked a color.
- **Notifications**: Classic cards get rounded corners; the Pill is shorter (440 x 62), with no dead space.
- **F7 test** (Settings > Advanced): 20 seconds to find out whether the app can see the F7 key at all.
