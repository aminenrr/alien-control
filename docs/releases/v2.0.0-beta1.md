**Alien Controls 2.0.0-beta1** (pre-release): the new 2.0 look. Every 1.3.5 feature, in a new tabbed window. The in-game overlay is not in this build: it comes in **2.0.0-beta2**.

### What's new
- **Tabs**: Home, Tuning, Lights and Overlay in one fixed 440 x 820 window. Every tab has the same height.
- **Home**: sensors and power, preset tiles in their colours, a G-Mode card with a switch, the lighting colours and brightness.
- **Tuning**: performance mode, GPU mode, GPU overclock with a new **G-MODE / + PERF** pill (same setting as the 1.3.4 Performance toggle), CPU boost, refresh rate and fans (**Auto / Max**).
- **Lights**: the zones (Keyboard, Rear ring, Alienhead, Turn off) are now on the page, plus colour, effect and brightness, the backlight timeout (moved here from Settings) and the status keys.
- **Settings**: one page with two tabs, General · Keys and Alerts · Advanced. Back arrow or Esc returns. Run on startup stays in the footer.
- **F3** opens and hides the app. **F4** is reserved for the overlay (beta 2).
- The header preset button hides on Home. Elsewhere a click cycles the presets like F2, a right click opens the list.
- Lighter: sensors are only read while Home or Tuning is on screen. Hidden, nothing polls.

### Removed
- The Fluent theme (Legend only now). Your old theme setting is ignored, everything else carries over.
- The compact view and its Full menu / Compact button.

### Overlay: coming in beta 2
The Overlay tab is there so you can see the layout, but it is disabled ("Coming in beta 2"). The overlay itself (PresentMon frametimes, levels 1 to 4 like MangoHud, F4 to toggle) ships in 2.0.0-beta2.
