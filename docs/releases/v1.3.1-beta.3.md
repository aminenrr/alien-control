**Alien Controls 1.3.1-beta.3**: Lights menu fixes from the beta.2 test. Untested pre-release.

### Changes
- Fix: turning the **Keyboard** off only turned off the white status keys; now the whole keyboard goes dark (and stays dark after ring / Alienhead changes, effects and the backlight timeout).
- The fourth tile reads **Turn off** / **Turn on**. It's in the accent color while lights are on, plain once everything is off.
- No colored outline left on a tile after clicking it.
- F7 can't be seen by the app (F7 test: nothing changes), so the Keyboard tile follows your taps only. F7 still sets the level.
