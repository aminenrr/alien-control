**Alien Controls 2.0.0-beta1.3-ov.5** (overlay preview, pre-release): on top of **beta1.3**, without the beta1.4 fixes. Includes everything from [ov.4](https://github.com/aminenrr/alien-control/releases/tag/v2.0.0-beta1.3-ov.4) (the Defender fix).

### New in ov.5
- **Level 1**: FPS, then the 1% low.
- **Level 2**: **RR** (refresh rate) after battery.
- **Level 3**: a **1% LOW** row.
- **Level 4**: 1% low (with max frametime) and a **0.1% LOW** row.
- Lows are the average of the slowest 1% / 0.1% of the last 2048 frames (about 8 s at 240 fps, 34 s at 60 fps). They come from the frames PresentMon already sends: no new source, no allocation, one small sort per update.
- RR reuses the level 4 refresh read (EnumDisplaySettings once every 5 s), on the timer level 2 already runs for battery.
