**Alien Controls 1.3.1**: new Lights menu, an all-lights shortcut and a restyled tray menu.

### New
- **Lights menu**: the laptop button opens four tiles: **Keyboard**, **Rear ring** and **Alienhead** (a lit tile means that light is on, tap to switch it) and **Turn off** / **Turn on** for all three. The power button is never touched.
- **Ctrl + Alt + F**: one shortcut for all three lights, in F7's order: full, off, 50% (half of the app's brightness), full. The tiles follow and a short notification shows the step.
- **Tray menu**: each preset shows its own icon. The selected preset's icon is in the accent color, and each name is in its preset's color. Rounded corners, the app's font and a rounded hover highlight in both themes, with no check mark.
- **Notifications**: a smaller Pill (440 x 62) and rounder Classic cards.

### Fixes
- Turning the keyboard off now turns off the whole keyboard and keeps it off after ring / Alienhead changes, colors, effects and F2.
- Turning the keyboard back on restores the five white status keys right away.
- One click on a tile is one action (no on-then-off double toggle). No colored outline after clicking a tile.
- Spectrum / Rainbow restart properly after the lights come back.

### Tested on the m15 R7 (betas 1 to 4)
- Lights menu, keyboard off/on, status keys, notifications, power button kept at its firmware color, tray idle behavior.
- **F7** (keyboard brightness) is handled by the laptop's firmware and sends nothing Windows can see, on any HID collection. The app can't follow it, so the Keyboard tile only follows the app.
- The beta test switches (Log frame gaps, F7 test) are removed.
