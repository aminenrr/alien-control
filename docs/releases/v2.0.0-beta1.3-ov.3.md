**Alien Controls 2.0.0-beta1.3-ov.3** (overlay preview, pre-release): on top of **beta1.3**, without the beta1.4 fixes. Includes everything from [ov.2](https://github.com/aminenrr/alien-control/releases/tag/v2.0.0-beta1.3-ov.2).

### New in ov.3
- **Each level keeps its own look**: position, size, background, move left / right and move up / down are saved per level (1 to 4). Switch levels with F4 or the slider and the overlay jumps to that level's spot.
- The Overlay tab edits the level that's on, or the last level while off. The Position line says which (for example "Top centre · level 3").
- **Reset to SteamOS** resets only the level you're editing. The other levels keep their look.
- Shared by all levels: update speed and the Latency and frame gen toggle.
- Your ov.2 look is copied to all four levels on first start.
