**Alien Controls 2.0.0-beta1.2** (pre-release): three fixes on top of beta1.1. Everything else is the same as beta1: the new 2.0 look with all 1.3.5 features. The in-game overlay comes in beta 2.

### Fixed
- **Live strips**: on Tuning the fans value ran into the PWR label. The strip now reads **CPU | GPU | PWR | FANS**, fans on the far right, with even gaps and a thin divider between each. The Home PWR strip (PWR | CPU | GPU | Total) gets the same dividers instead of the dots.
- **GPU overclock G-MODE / + PERF**: they were one either/or choice, so lighting + PERF switched G-MODE off. They are now two separate switches: either one, both, or neither. Both off = the overclock never runs, even with offsets kept. Your setting carries over (G-MODE on, + PERF as you had it). The G-Mode toast and the Home G-Mode card follow the switches.
- **Always opens on Home**: F3, the tray icon and the tray menu used to bring the window back on the last tab. It now always opens on Home (Settings closes too).
